# Week-5-Roblox-Build-Day
Plan It:

What is the goal of the website?

To advertise a day-long Roblox coding event to schools, students, and parents.

Who are your main visitors, and what do they care about?

Parents of students. They want to know that their child is learning something.

What is the one big idea your website should communicate about Build Day?

They will learn how to code there.

What will participants learn or make? What will they walk away with?

They will make a game.

Why should people who normally just play Roblox games want to participate in Build Day?

They will learn the basics of game development using the Roblox official language.

How should you split your website up? (ex. "What is Build Day?", "How it Works", "Sign Up", etc.)

Include a day schedule, and why people should sign up.

Test It:
Add images of a day at the event at the top of the page. Make it clear that this is a Roblox event. Make it more convincing why a person would sign up.

Expand It:
Challenge and teamwork-based. Not a gaming environment.
