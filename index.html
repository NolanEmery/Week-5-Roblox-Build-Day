Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Do I need coding experience?
Not at all! This workshop is designed for complete beginners. We'll guide you through everything step by step.
What should my child bring?
Just themselves and enthusiasm! We provide all laptops and materials. Feel free to bring a water bottle.
Is lunch included?
Yes! Pizza, snacks, and drinks are provided throughout the day. Let us know about any dietary restrictions on the sign-up form.
What age group is this for?
The workshop is designed for parents and children ages 6–17. Activities are adapted to different age and skill levels.
Is there a cost?
The workshop is $45 per family (parent + child). This covers all materials, lunch, snacks, and take-home resources.
